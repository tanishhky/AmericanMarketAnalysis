# American Market Analysis

An exploratory data science and quantitative finance analysis of American equity markets, specifically evaluating sector clusters and market capitalization traits.

![Market Clusters / Analysis Visualization]([Insert Image/Result Here])

## Overview

This repository focuses on breaking down the American stock market into distinct industry clusters to observe macroeconomic trends, correlations, and localized market cap behaviors. Utilizing extensive historical equity data, the notebooks aim to extract underlying patterns driving sector out-performance.

## Repository Structure
- `main_sector_clusters.ipynb`: Comprehensive clustering and exploratory data analysis grouping subsets of the market by volatility, growth, or size metrics.
- `getskrmktcap_sector.ipynb`: Scripts and analysis specific to calculating, tracking, and aggregating market capitalization weights on a per-sector basis.

## Usage
Ensure you have the typical PyData stack (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-Learn, Matplotlib) installed before launching the notebooks in Jupyter to reproduce the cluster models and structural analyses.
